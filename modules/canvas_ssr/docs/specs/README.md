# Specs

The specs say how canvas_ssr behaves, at three levels of detail. To use a
photography metaphor, think of these as your wide-angle (20mm), standard (50mm),
and telephoto (135mm) lenses:

- The [overview](overview/spec.md) covers the whole module in one page, in
  [OpenSpec](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec) format.
- The [semi-detailed specs](semi-detailed/) cover one area each, in OpenSpec
  format.
- The fully detailed specs are the [tests](../../tests/src/).
