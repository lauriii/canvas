---
title: Styling components
---

When component styles are enabled, a selected component's settings have two
tabs: **Content** and **Style**. The Style tab lists the styles the component
accepts, such as background, spacing, or alignment. Your site's design owner
decides which styles and options exist.

## Set and remove a style

1. Select a component in the preview or in Layers.
2. Open the **Style** tab. The tab badge shows how many styles are set.
3. Choose an option. The preview updates. In a list, hovering over or arrowing
   to an option previews it before you choose.
4. To unset a style, click the selected option again, choose **(Unset)** in a
   list, or click the style's name and choose **Reset**. Option-click (Alt-click)
   on the name resets it directly. An unset style shows no selection; there is
   no "Default" option.

A set style's name is highlighted. Click a name to read the style's
description. After you open Style once, other components you select open on
Style too, so you can style several components in a row.

Changes made in quick succession to the same style are undone in one step.

## Numbers you can type or drag

Some styles also take a number, such as a headline size or a width, if you have
permission to enter custom values. Type the number, or drag the slider, and the
preview updates as you go.

- Press the Up and Down arrow keys to change the number by one step, and hold
  Shift for ten steps.
- Drag the style's name left or right to change the number.
- The menu in the field switches the unit, keeping the size (4 rem becomes
  64 px), and lists the style's options.

## Style parts of a component

Some components have parts that take styles of their own, such as a hero's
heading and introduction. Each part has its own group in the Style tab, after
the component's own styles, named for the part.

- Hover over a part's group to outline the part in the preview.
- With the component selected, click a part in the preview to jump to its
  group.

## Find a style

The component's own styles are grouped in categories, such as Color or Layout,
followed by one group for each part.

## Copy and paste styles

Open a component's menu (right-click it, or use the menu in Layers):

- **Copy styles** (`Cmd+Option+C`).
- **Paste styles** (`Cmd+Option+V`) replaces the component's styles with the
  copied ones.
- **Paste and merge styles** keeps styles the copied ones do not set.
- **Remove all styles**.

Pasting applies to every selected component. Styles a component does not support
are skipped, and a notice names them.

## Styles that are no longer available

If the theme changes, or a component stops supporting a style, the style stays
stored but no longer renders. A notice at the top of the settings panel links to
each affected component, and Layers marks them with a warning icon. On the Style
tab, choose **Replace** to pick one of the style's current options, or
**Remove**.

## Translations and permissions

- Styles are shared by all languages. In a translation, the Style tab is
  read-only and tells you which language to change it in.
- Changing styles requires the "Use component styles" permission. Without it,
  the Style tab is read-only.
